# AI Prompts Collection

A carefully curated list of the most effective prompts for writing, coding, marketing, productivity, creativity, and more.

## Categories

### Writing & Content Creation
- "You are a world-class copywriter. Write a highly engaging blog post about [topic] for [audience]. Use storytelling, practical examples, and end with a strong CTA."
- "Generate 10 catchy and SEO-friendly title ideas for a blog post about [topic]."

### Coding & Development
- "Act as a senior full-stack developer. Write clean, well-commented code for [task] using [language/framework]."
- "Review this code and suggest improvements: [paste code]"

### Marketing & Sales
- "Write 10 high-converting Instagram captions for [product] targeting [audience]."
- "Create a 5-email sales sequence to sell [product]."

### Productivity & Personal Growth
- "Break down my goal '[goal]' into small daily actionable tasks for the next 30 days."
- "Create an ideal daily routine for a [profession] who wants to achieve [goal]."

### Business & Entrepreneurship
- "Give me a complete go-to-market strategy for this business idea: [idea]."
- "Generate 15 creative business name ideas for [industry]."
